Motivation letter template

A letter is one column. It is not a CV with a sidebar. LibreCV keeps the two documents separate.

What goes on the page

  1. Your name and how to reach you.
  2. The place and the date.
  3. Who you are writing to, then a subject line.
  4. Two or three short paragraphs about the role, not your whole career.
  5. A closing and your name again.

One invented letter

Sam Ibarra

Visitor services

Harbour City, 31 October 2026

Museum of everyday design
Hiring desk

Subject: the visitor-services role

The person at the desk should be able to explain an exhibition without turning it into a lecture. I have done that work in a small gallery, and I want to do it for you.

I can keep a busy Saturday calm, and I can write the short signs that people actually read.

Yours sincerely,
Sam Ibarra

Sam Ibarra and the museum are invented. They show the order of the blocks.
