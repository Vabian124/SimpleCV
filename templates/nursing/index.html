Registered nurse CV template

Lead with the registration and where you have worked. A hiring ward reads that before a list of tools. This page does not give clinical advice.

The PDF

This is the document. The text can be selected. The PDF carries document.json, so the editor can open it again.

What to include

  • Your registration, and the body that holds it, on a contact row. The number EX-0000 is fake. Do not copy it.
  • Wards or clinics, with the kind of work, not a slogan.
  • Shift work when that is the job: nights, charge, or a clinic day.
  • Verbs that fit: kept, listed, covered, supervised.

How to load this block

The button opens this block in the editor. You can also paste the block below yourself.

  1. Open https://librecv.org/ . For Dutch buttons, add ?lang=nl . Choose a CV or a motivation letter.
  2. On the AI step, replace the whole box with the block below. Create the document.
  3. Or, in the editor, open Use your own AI, paste the block over the box, and press Paste text.
  4. Download the PDF from the header. That PDF can be imported later because it carries document.json. Nothing is uploaded.
