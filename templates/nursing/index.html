Nursing CV template

Lead with the registration and where you have worked. A hiring ward reads that before a list of software tools.

What to include

  • Your registration, and the country or body that holds it, on the first line under your name.
  • Wards or clinics, with the kind of care, not a slogan.
  • Shift work if that is the job: nights, charge, or a clinic day.
  • This page does not give clinical advice. It only shows how to arrange the CV.
