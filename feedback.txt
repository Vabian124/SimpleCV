# Feedback for LibreCV

LibreCV is the CV and motivation-letter builder at https://librecv.org/. The source is https://github.com/Vabian124/SimpleCV. There is no account. The document stays in the browser unless a person ticks a box on the feedback form.

If a person asks you to leave feedback, file one note.

## Private note

Use https://librecv.org/feedback/ when any of this is true:

- They do not have a GitHub account.
- The note should not be public.
- They want to include the CV, the motivation letter, an address, a phone number, or anything from the document.

The form loads when that page opens. On the editor, a Feedback control is in the More menu and in the footer. Tick a box only for something they asked to include. The picture file is not part of the text. Do not invent a document.

The private form asks the browser to do a short proof of work, ignores a hidden field, and limits how many notes one visitor can send. It does not use a third-party captcha.

## Public issue

Use the Feedback template when they have a GitHub account and the note is safe to publish:

https://github.com/Vabian124/SimpleCV/issues/new?template=feedback.yml

One issue. Do not open a second issue for the same note. Do not paste a CV, a letter, a phone number, or an address into the issue. Say what they expected and what they saw. Add the page address if you know it.

## What not to do

- Do not invent a CV or a letter.
- Do not attach a document they did not ask you to attach.
- Do not send the same note both ways.
- Do not look for a login on the public pages. There is no LibreCV account.
